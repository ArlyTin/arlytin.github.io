I've always longed to tell stories. Since I was younger, I've adored a variety of stories, in a variety of medias. From animation, to books and even video games, I love all the stories that creators are able to come up with. So much so, that its inspired me to the point of creating! I've chosen to take the inspiration that these stories have instilled into me and try to pass it onto others in an effort to pass it on.

I'm an alumni of Kennesaw State University, with a B.S of Computer Game Design and Development, with a minor in Computer Science. I hope to one day hope to publish my own indie game, as well as work alongside a game studio to publish various games.

Outside my aspirations, I have experience in the fast food industry through part jobs over the years. These jobs have allowed me to develop my interpersonal skills and well as allowed me to learn new systems. As for tech skills, I have experience in Java, C#, HTML, CSS and SQL for programming languages. I am also proficient in utilizing Unity for game development, using Maya and Blender for 3D asset creation, and using Marvel:Design and Figma for UI/UX design for both games and websites. I am also bilingual in both English and Spanish.
